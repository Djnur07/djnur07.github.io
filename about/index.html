About the artist

@ZelythMochi

Mochi Friend is a hand-drawn collection of cozy chibi keepers. Each one takes care of a little living mochi, always held close and never left behind.

Every Mochi shares the same pose and style. Only the traits change: background, outfit, hair, eyes, expression, accessory, and the mochi they hold. A few rare keepers are Legendary one-of-ones.
